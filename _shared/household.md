# household.md

Who is in this house, and what the week normally looks like.

This is the file that lets the system say "nobody has the 3:15 pickup on Wednesday" instead of just listing Wednesday's events. Without it, you get a calendar readout. With it, you get a briefing.

Replace everything in brackets. Delete the italics.

## Who is away, and how it is marked

*The system cannot guess this. Tell it exactly what to look for.*

Who is out of the house on a given day lives only in [which calendar], as all-day events that read [e.g. "OFFICE" or a name followed by a city].

If an event says someone is away, they are away. If there is no event, they are home.

If a whole week has no away events at all, say so once in the message rather than assuming everyone is home. An empty week is more often an empty calendar.

---

## The worked example

Here is the rule that earns this file its place.

> When both parents are marked away on the same day, the morning routine falls to the helper, starting at 6:45 instead of 8:45. Say so in that day's line, by name, with the time.

One sentence, and it turns a calendar clash into an instruction. That is the job of every line in this file.

## The test

Read your finished file and ask: could a competent stranger run my Tuesday from this?

If the answer is no, the missing thing is what to add next.
